About

I'm Lorena, a business analyst and UX/UI designer. I work across ERP, web, and mobile products — gathering real requirements from stakeholders and users, then designing the interfaces that make those requirements usable, not just documented.

That perspective shapes how I approach every project: talking directly with the people who'll use the product, mapping the real workflow before touching a single pixel, and designing interfaces that developers can actually build and non-technical users can actually understand.
