02 — Field Sales / Tablet

mSalesRep

A field-sales tablet app that lets reps browse partners, build orders from a live product catalogue, and manage documents and financials — all from one tablet in the field.

Role
Business Analyst & UX/UI Designer
Platform
Android tablet, landscape
Deliverables
Requirements, design system, UI

The problem

Reps visit 10+ partners a day, often standing, sometimes with poor signal, and need to check an account and build an order in seconds. The app had to bring partners, a product catalogue, order documents, and financials into one place — without slowing a rep down in the field.

How might we

make managing a partner visit simple enough to do in the field, not just at a desk?

My role

I gathered the requirements as business analyst, working from a wireframe specification and reference screenshots to understand exactly what data, tasks, and business rules the app needed to support — then designed the interface around that, screen by screen.

  • Built a consistent design system first — dark sidebar, card-based content, one accent color, two typefaces — applied consistently across the whole app instead of styling each screen separately
  • Chose a lime-green accent that stays legible and calm against the dark sidebar
  • Designed the navigation around what reps actually do in a day — partners, orders, catalogue, documents, financials

Visual direction

The interface splits navigation from content on purpose: a dark sidebar to move around, light cards to read and work with data, so the two never blur together. One accent color is reserved for anything actionable — everything else stays quiet, so the eye knows where to go.
